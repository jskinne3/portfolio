# Colophon

The colorful keyboard in the link preview image comes from a typing instruction chart for the TRS-80 computer, found in the [November 1983 issue](https://colorcomputerarchive.com/repo/Documents/Magazines/Color%20Computer%20Magazine/Color%20Computer%20Magazine%20-%208311%20-%20November%201983.pdf) of *The Color Computer Magazine*. The heading typeface &mdash; Eras, created by Albert Boton and Albert Hollenstein in 1976 &mdash; was also inspired by that magazine. Thank you to [colorcomputerarchive.com](https://colorcomputerarchive.com/), whoever you are.

This website was made with Eleventy. I typed it at first on a IBM Model M keyboard, and more recently on a Keychron Q11 QMK. The body text is duospaced.
